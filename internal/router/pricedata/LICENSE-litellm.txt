prices.json in this directory is a trimmed snapshot of LiteLLM's public model
price and context-window table:

    https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BerriAI/litellm/main/model_prices_and_context_window.json

It is redistributed under the MIT licence below, which is LiteLLM's own. Only
four fields per model are kept (input and output cost per token, context window,
provider) and only rows publishing a per-token price at all; nothing else is
altered. Refresh it with `go run . prices fetch`.
